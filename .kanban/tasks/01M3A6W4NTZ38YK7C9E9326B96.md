## What
Before `registry.load()`, `runner.start` throws `unknownAgent(available: [])`, and `AgentsTool.make` gives "No agents are installed now." with no hint that the host forgot `load()`.

- Add `AgentRegistry.isLoaded` (true after the first successful `load()` or `reload()`).
- `AgentRunner.start` throws a new `AgentRunnerError.catalogNotLoaded` before the first load. `AgentsTool.make` throws the same error.
- `Sources/FoundationModelsAgents/CLI/AgentsCLIOperations.swift` has a `switch` over `AgentRunnerError`: add the new case, with a text in `Tool/AgentsToolText.swift`.
